How it works

Position size is based on your chosen cash risk divided by the stop distance’s estimated value per lot.

Lots = Balance × Risk %Stop (pips) × Pip value

Estimated margin uses the pair price, standard 100,000-unit lots, and selected leverage.

Risk reminder

Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains. A stop order may not fill at its requested price during fast markets.
