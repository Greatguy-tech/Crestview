Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Demo privacy: This static demo has no account backend and does not transmit your preferences to a Crestview server.

1. Information this demo uses

Crestview does not ask you to register or submit personal information. Interface preferences such as theme, accent color, density, text size, and reduced-motion choice are saved in your browser’s local storage so they can be remembered on this device.

Values entered into demo calculators are processed in your browser to display estimates. They are not sent to a Crestview server by this static website.

2. Simulated market content

Prices, charts, signals, economic calendar entries, account balances, and trading bot performance are demonstration content. The demo does not have a broker connection and does not receive or transmit trading activity.

3. External websites

Some links open third-party websites, including TradingView. When you follow an external link, that provider may collect information under its own privacy policy. Crestview does not control third-party services.

4. Storage and your choices

You can clear Crestview preferences by selecting “Reset defaults” on the Settings page, or clear this site’s local storage through your browser. Browser settings may also allow you to restrict local storage. The demo does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

5. Children and sensitive information

This trading-themed demo is not intended to provide financial services to children. Do not enter passwords, payment information, account credentials, or other sensitive personal information into this demonstration.

6. Policy updates

This policy may be revised as the demo changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the demo after changes constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy.
